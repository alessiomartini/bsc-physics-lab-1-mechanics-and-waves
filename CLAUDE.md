# Claude Code instructions

## Project

Archive of mechanics and waves laboratory reports, measurements, and source
materials.

## Workflow

- Read `README.md` and inspect the relevant experiment before editing.
- Treat measurements, reports, and PDFs as authoritative historical artifacts.
- Do not alter data, units, uncertainty estimates, or conclusions without explicit approval.
- Verify that edited documents open and that referenced files remain present.
- Keep temporary exports out of git and inspect the diff before committing.
